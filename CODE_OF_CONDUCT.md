# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be kind. Assume good intent.

This is a small open project. Intent here is trying to make something genuinely useful. Treat people accordingly.

---

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate openly and respectfully
- Accept feedback graciously
- Focus on the problem, not the person
- Welcome contributors of all experience levels

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, abuse, or discrimination of any kind
- Deliberate intimidation or hostility
- Dismissing contributions without explanation

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate this code.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum associated with repoinfo.
